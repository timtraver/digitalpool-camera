1
Unpack & power on

Set up the hardware

  • Position each camera so it sees the whole table. Set it on a shelf or mount it using whatever mounting hardware came with the camera — whatever gives the cleanest view of the playing surface.
  • If you are using USB cameras, connect their USB cables to the USB ports on the camera unit. Either port works, and a second camera goes into any free one.
  • Optional but recommended: run an Ethernet cable from your router to the unit. This is the easiest way to get it online.
  • Plug in the power supply last. The unit powers on by itself — there is no power button to press.
  • Wait about 60 seconds. The device boots, starts its hotspot, and brings the cameras up.
Leave it plugged in. This device is designed to stay powered on around the clock so streams and recordings are always available. To shut it down cleanly, use ⚡ Power in the admin interface rather than pulling the plug.

Getting connectedSteps 2 – 3
2
Connect to the device

Join the camera's WiFi hotspot

  • On your laptop, tablet, or phone, open the WiFi list and look for a network named DigitalPool-XXXX, where XXXX is four characters unique to your unit. Join it.
  • Enter the hotspot password shown below.
  • The admin screen opens by itself a moment after you connect — there is no address to type in. If it doesn't appear, open a browser and go to http://192.168.50.1:3000
  • Sign in with the default administrator account.
  • You will be asked to set a new password immediately. Choose something you will remember — this is now your admin login for this device.

No internet over the hotspot. The hotspot connects you to the device, not to the internet. Your laptop may warn that the network has no connection — that is normal and expected. Step 3 is what gets the device itself online.
3
Get the device online

Give it an internet connection

In the admin interface, open the 📶 Network & WiFi Setup panel and choose one of these two routes.

🔗 Ethernet Easiest
  • Plug the cable from your router into the unit.
  • It picks up an address automatically — nothing to configure.
  • The panel shows the assigned IP address. Write it down.
📶 WiFi No cable
  • Open 📶 Connect to WiFi Network.
  • Pick your venue's network, type its password, connect.
  • Wait for it to report connected, then note the IP address.
Switch to the venue network now. Once the device has its own IP address, reconnect your laptop to that same network and browse to http://<device-ip>:3000. You will get a faster, more reliable connection than over the hotspot — and the hotspot stays available as a permanent backup way in.

Register & go liveSteps 4 – 5
4
Claim the device

Register it to your venue

  • Scroll to the Admin Settings area and open 📡 Device Registration.
  • The Device Name is fixed to this unit — you do not need to change it.
  • Enter your DigitalPool account email and password — the same login you use at digitalpool.com.
  • Press 🔗 Register Device.
  • If your account has more than one venue, pick the venue this camera belongs to and press ✅ Confirm Venue.
  • Success looks like a green ✓ Registered badge on the section heading.
Streaming stays disabled until this step is finished. Registration is what links the camera to your venue and turns on secure remote access, so it must be completed before anything will go live. It also needs a working internet connection — if you see 🔴 No Internet Connection, go back to Step 3.
Your DigitalPool password is used once to verify your account and is never stored on the device.
5
Frame the shot

Aim the cameras and start streaming

  • Use the 📷 Camera 1 / 📷 Camera 2 tabs at the top to switch between cameras. The live preview appears on the left.
  • Open 🎮 PTZ Controls. The outer arrows move in large steps, the inner arrows in fine steps, and the slider sets zoom. Frame the full table with a little room to spare.
  • Happy with the framing? Press 🏠 Set Home so the camera returns to this exact position every time it restarts.
  • If the picture needs work, open ⚙️ Camera Settings for exposure, white balance, and focus.
  • Open 🎥 Stream Server and press ▶ Start.
  • The status bar turns green and the live stats — FPS and bitrate — begin moving. Repeat for your second camera.
Leave the streams running. A running stream is what recording attaches to in Step 6. The device restarts its streams automatically after a power cut, so this is normally a one-time setup.

Recording matches & troubleshootingStep 6
6
You're live — now record

Scan the table QR code to record a match

  • Sign in at digitalpool.com and go to your venue settings area. This is where each of your tables and its QR code lives.
  • Get the QR codes for your tables from there — print them and put one at each table so players can reach them easily. You only have to do this once.
  • Players scan the QR code at their table with their phone, which opens that table's scoring screen and lets them create a match on that table.
  • Starting the match automatically tells the camera assigned to that table to begin recording the stream — nobody has to touch the camera unit.
  • When the match is finished, the recording is saved and attached to the match on digitalpool.com.
That's the whole day-to-day workflow. Steps 1 – 5 happen once, at install. After that: scan, play, done.

If something isn't working

SymptomWhat to try
The DigitalPool-XXXX hotspot doesn't appear Give it a full 60 seconds after power-up, then refresh your WiFi list. If it still doesn't show, unplug the power for 10 seconds and try again.
The admin screen didn't open on its own Open a browser and go to http://192.168.50.1:3000 — include the :3000. Also confirm you are joined to the camera's hotspot, not cellular data or the venue WiFi.
Registration says No Internet Connection The device itself is offline. Plug in Ethernet or connect it to WiFi in 📶 Network & WiFi Setup, then press 🔄 Check Again.
Registration is rejected Confirm the email and password work at digitalpool.com, and that your account has a venue. If it has none, create one there first, then register again.
Start does nothing / streaming is disabled The device is almost certainly not registered yet. Finish Step 4 and look for the green ✓ Registered badge.
Camera 2 tab is empty Check the second camera's USB cable, then reload the page in your browser.
Preview is black but stats look healthy Reload the page. If the preview stays black, check the camera's lens cap and USB cable — the stream itself may be fine.
Forgot the admin password Contact DigitalPool support — the device can be reset without losing your venue registration.
Need a hand? Support can connect to your device remotely once it is registered — no network changes needed on your side. Reach us through your venue dashboard at digitalpool.com and have your device name ready.
