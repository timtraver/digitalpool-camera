DuoCast Streaming Box

Quick Start
Guide

Six steps from a sealed box to a live, recording table stream. You set it up from the DigitalPool app on your phone, over Bluetooth. There's nothing to install on the box and no password to type into it.

1
Unpack & power on

Set up the hardware

  • Place or mount each camera where you expect it to cover the whole table. Use a shelf, or the mounting hardware that came with the camera. You'll see the picture and fine-tune the aim from your phone in Step 5.
  • Connect each camera's USB cable to the USB ports on the box. Any port works, and a second camera goes into any free one.
  • Optional but recommended: run an Ethernet cable from your router to the box. Any standard network cable works, and it's the easiest way to get the box online.
  • Plug in the power supply last. The box powers on by itself. There's no power button to press.
  • Wait about 60 seconds. The box starts up, brings the cameras up, and begins showing up over Bluetooth for your phone to find.
Using your own cameras? This guide covers the USB cameras that come with the box. Network (IP) cameras or other models may need extra steps to connect to the stream, so contact DigitalPool support first.
Leave it plugged in. The box is made to stay on around the clock. To shut it down, use ⚡ Power on its settings page (Step 5), not the plug.

Getting connectedSteps 2 – 3
2
Find it in the app

Connect to the box from your phone

  • Install the DigitalPool app and sign in with the account that manages your venue.
  • Turn on Bluetooth on your phone and stand within a few meters of the box.
  • On the home screen, tap Stream your matches, then DuoCast. Allow Bluetooth when the app asks for it.
  • Under Nearby Unregistered Boxes, your box appears as DP-DuoCast-XXXX with a New tag. XXXX is four characters unique to the box.
  • Tap it. If your phone asks to pair with the box, tap Pair.
In the app: Stream your matches› DuoCast› DP-DuoCast-XXXX
Setting up more than one box? The list puts the closest box first, with the most signal bars. Hold your phone next to the box you mean and pick the top one.
No hotspot and no passwords. The box doesn't make its own WiFi network, and you don't type a password into it. Your sign-in to the DigitalPool app is what tells the box who you are.
3
Get the box online

Set up an internet connection

The box's screen in the app shows its Device Network. Use one of these two routes.

🔗 Ethernet Easiest
  • Plug the cable from your router into the box.
  • There's nothing to set up: Ethernet shows Connected on its own.
  • Wait for Internet to show Online.
📶 WiFi No cable
  • Tap Set up WiFi and choose your venue's network.
  • Type its password and tap Connect.
  • Wait for "The box is online on your network."
Use the venue's main network, not a guest network. Many guest networks stop devices from talking to each other, so your phone won't be able to open the box's preview or settings. A network that makes you accept terms on a sign-in page won't work at all. Use Ethernet there.
Upload speed is what makes or breaks a stream. Each camera sends about 2–5 Mbps to the internet the whole time it streams, so two cameras need at least 10–15 Mbps of upload, all the time and not just at quiet moments. Run a speed test on the venue's network and look at the upload number, not download; many business connections have far less upload than download. If upload is too low, the stream stutters or drops, and a recording can be cut short.

Register & go liveSteps 4 – 5
4
Claim the box

Register it to your venue and table

  • On the box's screen, tap Register this box.
  • Choose which venue the box is at. If it isn't listed, tap Create a new venue.
  • Choose which table the box is filming, or tap Add a new table. This is what links a match on that table to this box's cameras in Step 6.
  • Wait while it says Connecting the box to DigitalPool… This can take a minute.
  • You're done when it says "The box is registered at your venue, your table." From then on, the box is listed under My DuoCast boxes.
Streaming stays off until this step is finished. Registering links the box to your venue and turns on secure remote support. It needs the internet connection from Step 3.
5
Frame the shot

Aim the cameras and start streaming

  • Under Camera Streams, tap Camera 1 Preview to see the live picture. Your phone needs to be on the same WiFi as the box for the preview to play.
  • Use the arrows to pan and tilt, and the zoom buttons to frame the whole table with a little room to spare. When you're happy, tap Set as Home so the camera returns to this spot every time the box starts.
  • Choose the camera's overlay and where it streams.
  • Tap Start stream. The camera changes to Streaming. Repeat for Camera 2.
Everything else is on the box's settings page. Tap Full Device Management to open it, already signed in. That's where you'll find picture settings (exposure, white balance, focus), other stream destinations, and ⚡ Power. On a computer, type the address the app shows into a browser. The computer needs to be on the same network as the box.
Leave the streams running. A running stream is what recording uses in Step 6. The box restarts its streams by itself after a power cut, so this is normally a one-time setup.

Recording matchesStep 6
6
You're live — now record

Start a match to start a recording

Starting a match on a table tells the box that films that table (the one you chose in Step 4) to start recording. Nobody has to touch the box. There are two ways to start a match:

📱 Table QR code
  • Sign in at digitalpool.com and open your venue settings, where each table has its own QR code.
  • Print them and put one at each table. You only do this once.
  • Players scan the code at their table, which opens its scoring screen, and create a match there.
🖥 Scoring tablet
  • If the table has a DigitalPool scoring tablet, start the match on the tablet as usual.
  • Make sure the tablet is set to the same table the box films.
  • When the match is over, the recording is saved and attached to the match on digitalpool.com.
That's the whole day-to-day workflow. Steps 1 – 5 happen once, at install. After that: start a match, play, done.

Moving the box, or changing the venue's WiFi

If the box goes offline because it moved or the WiFi password changed, you can fix it from the app over Bluetooth, even with no network. Stand near it, open DuoCast, and tap the box under My DuoCast boxes. Then, under Device Network, tap Change and choose the network again.

Need a hand? Once the box is registered, support can connect to it remotely, with no network changes needed on your side. Reach us through your venue dashboard at digitalpool.com and have the box's name (DP-DuoCast-XXXX) ready.

TroubleshootingIf something isn't working
SymptomWhat to try
The box doesn't appear under Nearby Unregistered Boxes Give it a full 60 seconds after power-up, check that Bluetooth is on, move within a few meters, and tap Search again. A box that's already registered to you is listed under My DuoCast boxes instead.
The box shows In use by another phone Another phone has the box open in the app. Close the box's screen on that phone, then try again.
The box is under Registered to Another Account It belongs to a different DigitalPool account. Sign in with that account, or ask its owner to remove the box so you can register it.
iPhone says it has an out-of-date copy of the box's Bluetooth settings Open Settings › Bluetooth, tap ⓘ next to the box, choose Forget This Device, then try again.
Wrong password when joining WiFi Check the password and try again. The box keeps whatever connection it already had until the new one works.
The box joined WiFi but can't reach the internet The network probably needs a sign-in page, which the box can't get through. Use Ethernet or a different network.
Registering says your sign-in has expired Sign out of the DigitalPool app and back in, then tap Register this box again.
The preview won't play, or Full Device Management won't open Your phone must be on the same network as the box: not mobile data, and not a separate guest network. On iPhone, also check Settings › DigitalPool › Local Network. If it still won't open, the router is stopping devices from talking to each other. The venue's router settings can change that.
Start stream does nothing The box isn't registered yet (finish Step 4), or the camera has no destination set (Step 5).
A camera says No camera connected Check that camera's USB cable at both ends, then go back and open the box again.
Preview is black but the camera shows Streaming Check the camera's lens cap and USB cable. The stream itself may be fine.
